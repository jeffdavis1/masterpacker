## Value

Pack smarter, not harder.

MasterPacker takes the guesswork out of packing. Tell it who's going, how you're getting there, and what you'll be doing — it builds a personalized packing list in seconds, then keeps everyone in sync from the first day of planning to the moment you're finally unpacked.

**Smart suggestions from day one**

Add your travelers (and pets), pick your activities — hiking, a business trip, the beach — and MasterPacker builds your starting packing list automatically. It checks the weather at your destination too, so you're not caught without a jacket or an umbrella.

**Never forget the basics**

Save each traveler's — and pet's — go-to essentials once. From then on, they're added to every new trip automatically. No re-typing your toothbrush and phone charger every single time.

**Built for how you actually pack**

Going skiing? Hitting the gym on a business trip? Build a reusable bag for any purpose and add it to any trip in one tap — MasterPacker remembers what goes in it so you don't have to.

**Organize your way**

See your packing list grouped by traveler, or by which suitcase or carry-on each item belongs in. Switch views anytime, whatever makes sense for how you're actually packing that day.

**Pack together**

Traveling with family or friends? Share a trip so everyone can add, check off, and pack from the same list — no more "did you pack the sunscreen?" texts.

**No sign-up. No account. Just packing.**

MasterPacker uses your existing iCloud account to keep everything synced across your own devices. There's nothing to set up and nothing extra to remember.

Whether it's a weekend getaway or a two-week trip, MasterPacker makes sure you show up with everything you need — and nothing you don't.
