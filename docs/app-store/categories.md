# App Store Categories

**Field:** Categories  
**Limit:** 1 Primary + 1 Secondary (optional)  
**Updated:** 2026-09-03

## Primary Category

```
Travel
```

**Rationale:** Trip planning, destination-focused features, vacation use case

## Secondary Category (Optional)

```
Productivity
```

**Rationale:** Organization, list-making, sharing/collaboration features, personal productivity

---

## Category Positioning

| Aspect | Travel | Productivity |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| **User Intent** | Planning a trip | Organizing information |
| **Feature Fit** | Destination, weather, trip dates | Packing lists, reusable templates, sharing |
| **Primary Appeal** | Vacation/travel planners | Power organizers |
| **Discovery** | Users searching "travel" category | Users searching "productivity" category |

---

## Notes

- Travel is the primary category (most important for App Store ranking)
- Productivity as secondary captures users looking for organizational tools
- This positioning ensures visibility in both travel and productivity searches
- Categories cannot be changed after launch; confirm before submission
